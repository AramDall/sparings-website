Privacy Policy

We take your privacy seriously. Sparings runs entirely offline — your transactions, accounts, and budgets are stored only on your device and never sent to our servers. We do not collect, track, share, or sell your personal or financial data.

Two exceptions worth knowing about: if you choose to back up your data, that backup is created and shared entirely through your device's own sharing tools (like Google Drive or Files) — we never see or store it. If you subscribe to Sparings Premium, our payment partner RevenueCat and Google Play process your purchase and manage your subscription status on our behalf; they receive limited technical information (such as a purchase receipt and device identifier) needed to verify your subscription — never your financial transaction data.

The app may request permission for Face ID/fingerprint (to lock the app) and notifications (for payment reminders) — both stay entirely on your device.
